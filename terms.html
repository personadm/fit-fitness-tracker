What fin is

fin is an app for recording and analysing training. You create training plans, log sets, repetitions and weights, see your history in charts, and can share completed sessions with people who follow you.

With fin Pro, fin also imports workouts that other apps have stored in Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) into your fin history: runs, rides and swims automatically, unless you switch this off in the settings, and other sessions one at a time when you tap them.

The range of features continues to develop. There is no entitlement to the continued existence of individual features as long as the core of the service remains.
