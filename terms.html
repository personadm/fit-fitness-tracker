2. What fin offers

  • Training: creating plans, logging sets, reps and weights, history and charts, suggestions for progression, a coach that suggests plans, and a training readiness value.
  • Nutrition: a calorie and nutrient target based on your details, a food diary, a food database and recognition of meals from photos, speech or text using AI (section 9).

fin does not provide medical advice, nutritional counselling, personal coaching or purchasing advice. All values are calculated guide values (section 8).
