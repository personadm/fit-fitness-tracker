WHOOP integration

Design of the WHOOP integration in fin (iOS and Android), built and tested with a WHOOP member in Sandbox. Values are examples.

fin is a training app for strength, runs, food and daily readiness. fin is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.

How it works

  • OAuth 2.0 with the token exchange on our server (Supabase Edge Functions, EU). Client secret and tokens never reach the phone.
  • Webhooks v2. Every call is checked against X-WHOOP-Signature before fin fetches the record.
  • Read only. fin asks for read scopes plus offline for token refresh, and never writes to WHOOP.
  • WHOOP data is private by default. It never shows in the feed or rankings unless the member opts in.
  • No sale of data, no ads, no AI training with WHOOP data.
  • Disconnect calls DELETE /v2/user/access and deletes all imported WHOOP data.
