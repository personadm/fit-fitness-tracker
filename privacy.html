  • Your training and nutrition data is health data. We only process it with your explicit consent.

  • You buy fin Pro through the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details.
  • Health data is never used for advertising.

Overview

fin records and analyses your training and your nutrition. In doing so we process data that qualifies as a special category of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR – in particular your training, nutrition and body data.

For this data we obtain explicit, separate consent. Without it, the core function of the app cannot be used.

Some values from Apple Health and Health Connect stay exclusively on your device; others are stored with your account. Which ones is set out in Section 8 and Section 9.

We do not show ads, we do not track for advertising purposes, and we do not sell data. In your profile we link to partners (see “Partner links”). We do not embed any advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services. On Android, fin contains three components from Google that themselves send technical information to Google: ML Kit for the barcode scanner and text recognition, Google Play Billing, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Details are under “Recipients”. How often the purchase page leads to a purchase is something we evaluate ourselves (see Section 13).

  • On Android, fin uses the system’s speech recognition. From Android 13 onwards it recognises on the device. AI features

AI analysis of meals and recipes

When you take a photo of your meal or of a nutrition label, speak or type a sentence about your food, have a product looked up, or take over a recipe from TikTok, fin can analyse this with an AI. This only happens if you have agreed in the “Before fin asks an AI” sheet, separately for each of these kinds.

In that case we send the following via our server (Supabase, EU) to OpenAI (OpenAI Ireland Ltd., Dublin, Ireland):

  • the photo (re-encoded, without location data or other image metadata),
  • the spoken or typed sentence,
  • for a product lookup, brand and product name; OpenAI searches the web for this,
  • for a TikTok import, the description of the video. Our server retrieves the video page from TikTok; TikTok sees only our server’s address, not your device.

Your name, your email address, your account ID and your health or body values are not sent to OpenAI. OpenAI does not use the data for training and stores it for no more than 30 days for abuse detection. Processing takes place in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) in the data processing agreement with OpenAI.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; where health data is concerned, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it in the settings at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out up to that point.

Sharing from TikTok

If you share a TikTok video to fin, fin only stores the link on your device. It is analysed only in the app, as described above.

Apple Health

fin can work together with Apple Health if you want it to. This connection is voluntary; without it the app works fully, but certain analyses remain empty.

What fin reads from Health

Only if you enable the connection in settings and grant permission in the iOS authorisation dialog:

  • Body weight – only if you additionally switch on its own toggle
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability
  • Steps and active energy
  • Sleep data
  • Workouts recorded by other apps, for endurance sessions including the waypoints of their route
  • Cardio fitness (VO2 max), running and cycling power, stride length and cadence, where available

What happens to this data

Not all of this data is treated the same way. Some of it stays on your device, some is stored with your fin account on our servers. We state this for each kind individually.

Recovery values, steps and routes stay on your device. Sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and your steps for the day are processed locally only, serve to display your recovery values and your steps, and are not transmitted to us. The same applies to the waypoints of a route, see “Route and map” below.

Stored with your account are the heart rate and calories of a session and, if you switch it on, your body weight.

Heart rate and active energy for a workout

For a workout in your fin history, fin reads the heart rate from the period of that workout – average, maximum and curve – and the active energy burned during it. fin stores both with that entry.

Your history is stored with your account on our servers so that it is the same on all your devices – and so are these values. Other users do not see your heart rate. People who follow you see the calorie count of an endurance session unless you set the session to “Only me” (see Section 10).

Body weight

fin only takes over your body weight if you additionally switch on the Take body weight from Health toggle in the settings. It is off by default. When you switch it on, you choose whether measurements from the last 30 days come along or only those from that moment on.

After that, fin takes over every weight measurement that arrives in Health – for example from a scale whose app writes to Health. For each measurement fin stores the weight, the time and the name of the app it came from.

So that a new measurement arrives even when you do not have fin open, Apple Health may briefly launch fin in the background. fin then reads only the new weight measurements and gets them ready on your device, nothing else.

fin treats measurements taken over in this way like a body weight you entered yourself. They are stored with your account on our servers and synchronised between your devices. Body weight is used for your weight curve, for calculating your calorie requirement and for the strength rating on the leaderboard.

The strength rating uses your best sets of a completed week, your most recently stored weight and your stated sex. fin calculates it in the app, at launch and after every workout. If the app has not updated your rating since the start of the week, for example because you have not opened fin, our server recalculates it at night: from the data stored in your account (your training log, your body weight and your stated sex) and by the same rules as the app. This data does not leave our servers at Supabase (EU) in the process. So that we do not read your account again every night, we remember until the end of the week which state of it we read. Other users see only the result, i.e. your tier and score – namely people who follow you and, if your profile is public, other users as well. No other user sees your body weight itself.

If you delete a measurement in fin, fin will not take it over again. For this, fin remembers the time and identifier of the deleted measurement in your account. The measurement remains in Apple Health; you delete it there in the Health app. Conversely, if a measurement that was taken over is deleted in Health while the takeover is switched on, fin also removes it from your history at the next sync.

Workouts from other apps

fin takes over workouts such as runs, rides and swims into your history automatically, including those from the last seven days. You can switch this off in the settings with the Take workouts automatically toggle.

Walks and older sessions are shown to you in a list in the history view. There you decide for each individual session, by tapping it, whether it is added to your history. Workouts that fin recorded itself are not taken over a second time.

This also applies to sessions from your Garmin watch that Garmin Connect stores in Apple Health. fin does not exchange any data with Garmin itself.

A session that is taken over becomes an ordinary fin entry and is stored with your history in your account on our servers. Unlike a workout recorded in fin, however, it does not appear in the feed by itself: people who follow you only see it if you share it yourself (see Section 10).

If you share it, the following is visible: the sport type – or, for a sport type fin cannot assign, the name of the recording app instead – plus time and duration, distance covered and pace where applicable, and the calorie count if Health provides one. Others do not see heart rate, sleep or weight data, or maps and route points.

Route and map

For running, cycling and other endurance sessions with a route, fin reads the waypoints (coordinates, altitude, pace) from Apple Health to display the map and the course of the route. This also applies to sessions that other apps or watches have stored in Apple Health. The waypoints stay on your device. They are neither stored in your account nor passed on to other users; only you see the map, in your history – unless you share it yourself as an image (see Section 11).

To draw the map image, your iPhone requests from Apple Maps (Apple Inc.) the map section that encloses the route. Apple receives only this section, not the route itself. This happens when you open a session, automatically for up to three sessions that have just been taken over and, when you open a day in your history, for up to three sessions of that day. This only happens while fin is open.

Other values

fin also reads cardio fitness (VO2 max), running and cycling power, stride length and cadence.

What fin writes to Health

If you allow it, fin stores the workouts you record in the app as sessions in Health so that your activity there is complete. You can revoke this permission at any time.

Legal basis

Health data is processed solely on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) in conjunction with Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You give it by granting permission in the iOS authorisation dialog. For body weight, the separate toggle in fin is also required. For visibility to others see Section 10.

Withdrawal

  • You withdraw the Health permission in the iOS settings under Privacy & Security → Health → fin. fin will then read no new data.
  • You end the takeover of body weight with the toggle in fin. fin then no longer reads body weight from Health and is no longer launched in the background for this. The toggle applies to your account: on other devices it takes effect the next time fin is opened there. Measurements already taken over remain in your history until you delete them there.
  • You delete sessions that have already been taken over in fin like any other workout. This also removes them from your account on our servers and, if you shared them, from the feed.
  • Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out up to that point.

We use Health data exclusively within the app. It is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining – this is additionally prohibited by Apple’s App Review Guideline 5.1.3. Health data is not stored in iCloud.

If we later extend the scope of permissions, we will ask for your explicit consent again.

Health Connect (Android)

On Android devices fin does not work with Apple Health but with Health Connect – the system’s health data store, which scales, watches and other fitness apps write to. This connection is voluntary as well; without it the app works fully, but certain analyses remain empty.

The same rules apply as in the previous section. Sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, the day’s steps and the waypoints of a route stay on the device, as do the elevation gain, power and cardio fitness of a session. Body weight, sessions that are taken over, and heart rate and active energy for a workout are stored with your account. The same toggles exist, and Section 10 applies to the visibility of a session: others only see sessions that were taken over if you share them yourself.

What fin reads from Health Connect

Only after you grant permission in the Health Connect authorisation dialog:

  • Body weight – only with its own toggle
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (RMSSD)
  • Steps and active energy
  • Sleep data
  • Distance covered
  • Workouts recorded by other apps
  • The route (waypoints) of an endurance session recorded by other apps – only on the device, for the map, laps and elevation profile. No coordinate is stored with your account. You grant permission for this in Health Connect, for each route individually or permanently.
  • Elevation gain, power (watts) and cardio fitness (VO2 max) for such a session – for display only; they stay on the device.

About heart rate variability

Apple Health provides it as SDNN, Health Connect as RMSSD. These are two different measures of the same thing; their figures cannot be converted into one another. fin therefore only compares them with your own history on the same device and never across platforms.

About body weight

On Android, Health Connect does not launch fin in the background. fin reads new weight measurements only when you open the app or return to it, and does not request any additional permission for this. As the source of a measurement, fin stores the name of the app that wrote it to Health Connect.

If a measurement that was taken over is deleted in Health Connect, fin only learns of this if it is opened at least every 30 days during that time. If fin misses a deletion in this way, you remove the measurement in fin yourself.

What fin writes to Health Connect

If you allow it: the workouts you record in the app, as an exercise session (sport type, start, duration). If you entered calories yourself for an endurance session, that value is added. fin does not write an estimate. Nothing more.

Route and map on Android

On Android, fin draws the map itself, using map material from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org, data from OpenStreetMap). For this, your device loads the map tiles for the section shown – that is, the rectangle that encloses the route, not the individual waypoints. As with any request on the web, OpenFreeMap receives your device’s IP address, but it requires no sign-in and no key and sets no cookies. None of this is sent to us. Waypoints and the finished map image stay on the device. fin does not query your location on Android.

fin loads the tiles when you open a session and, for some sessions that have just been taken over or that you open in your history, also in advance so that the map is ready there. This only happens while fin is open.

Withdrawal on Android

You withdraw permission in Health Connect → App permissions → fin – on devices up to Android 13 in the “Health Connect” app, from Android 14 in the system settings. fin will then read no new data. You switch off body weight on its own with the toggle in fin. You delete sessions and measurements that have already been taken over in fin like any other entry.

No advertising, no selling, no passing on. Health data from Health Connect is not used for advertising, not sold and not passed on to third parties. The only exception is the map image of a route: for this, OpenFreeMap learns the map section, never the waypoints (see “Route and map on Android”). Only what is described above as “with your account” is kept on our servers. Of this, only your sessions become visible to other users, under the rules in Section 10 – sessions that were taken over only if you share them yourself – and your tier on the leaderboard.

Social features

fin includes features that let you connect with other people.

Search and following

Others find you via your username or display name. We process username, display name, profile picture, bio and the following relationships. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

By default, any signed-in person can follow you without confirmation. Under Settings → Who sees what → Private profile you can change this so that you have to confirm every follow request.

Feed

People who follow you see your completed sessions unless you set visibility to “Only me” (in general under Settings → Who sees what, or when you finish an individual session). Visible are the name, duration, exercises, sets, weights and total load, and for endurance sessions also distance, pace and calories burned. Others never see maps or route points. Sessions that fin has taken over from Apple Health or Health Connect only appear in the feed if you share them yourself.

Missed days

If you switch on Account & Settings → Who sees what → Report missed days, your followers sometimes learn the next day that you skipped a planned training day. Rest days and sick days do not count. It is off by default. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR – your consent by switching it on; you withdraw it with the same toggle.

Comments and reactions

Data processed: the text of your comments, reactions given, the time, and the association with your account and the session in question.

Follow requests

If a person has set their profile to private and you want to follow them, a request is stored and shown to that person – with your username, your profile picture and the time. For all other profiles the following relationship is created immediately. We store accepted, declined and pending requests until the relationship ends or one of the two accounts is deleted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

In-app notifications

We store records that someone has followed you, commented on your session or reacted to it, so that we can show them to you in the app. This involves processing the person who triggered it, the occasion, the reference to the session and the time.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete read notifications after 90 days.

Training partners

You can add others to a session as training partners. The link between the accounts is stored and shown in the session. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and with regard to the health-related aspects Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.

Ongoing workout

While you are training, the top of your followers’ feed shows that you are at it right now: the sport type and name of the workout and since when. For this we store a row with these details; when you finish the workout, we delete it. Your followers can cheer you on once while you train (see Section 12). Under Settings → Who sees what you switch this off with Others see when I train. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Rest day requests

If you want to take an extra rest day on a day on which training is actually planned, you ask your friends – people you follow who also follow you. They see your request in the feed, if you like with a short reason (up to 80 characters), and vote yes or no. We store the request with the day, deadline and reason, and the votes with the account of the person voting. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete requests and votes together with the respective account.

Shout-outs on the leaderboard

On the leaderboard you can send a short shout-out (1 to 80 characters) to a person you follow who also follows you, or whose profile is public. It appears next to your name for that person. We store sender, recipient, week, text and time; only the two of you can read it. If you write to the same person again, the new shout-out replaces the old one. As with comments, we check shout-outs for prohibited words. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. A shout-out is deleted at the latest when one of the two of you deletes their account.

Invitations and referral reward

You can invite friends to fin via your device’s share menu. The invitation contains your invitation code, which is your username. If someone enters it when registering, we store who invited whom, when, and whether the invited person has completed their first session – only then does the invitation count.

For invitations that count, you receive free time for fin Pro. On iPhone you receive it as an offer code from Apple that we assign to you. On Android we credit it to you: if a subscription is running through Google Play, we ask Google to postpone the next charge by these days; otherwise we unlock fin Pro for these days ourselves. For this we store how many days you have earned and how many have been credited, as well as the codes and credits assigned. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete this data together with your account. An offer code assigned to you then remains in our stock without any link to your account, so that it is not issued a second time.

Reporting and blocking

You can report sessions, comments and accounts, and block individual accounts. For a report we process the identifier of the reported account, the reported content, your identifier, the reason given and the time. We store a block as a relationship between the two accounts; blocked people no longer see your content and cannot follow you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in a platform free of abuse – and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR to fulfil obligations under the Digital Services Act.

If you delete your account, the reports and blocks you have submitted are deleted as well. The reference to reported content is removed as soon as that content no longer exists.

Sharing content

The share function creates graphics: for a session, summarising your workout, your volume or the muscle groups you trained, and for sessions with a route also with the map or just the outline of your loop; for a recipe, as a nutrition label; and from the fin tile on your Home screen.

The graphics are created on your device. They are not transmitted to us. You pass a graphic on via your device’s share menu, save it to your photos (on Android to the gallery, in the album fin) or copy it. When saving, fin does not read your other photos; on iPhone, fin only asks for permission to add images, on Android 8 and 9 for permission to write to storage. As soon as you share a graphic in another app or on a social network, that provider’s privacy terms apply. Since the graphics contain training or nutrition data, consider the audience before sharing.

Notifications

Training and meal reminders

fin schedules training reminders and the reminders for breakfast, lunch and dinner locally on your device. Whether a meal has already been logged that day, so that its reminder is skipped, is checked by your device itself. No data is transmitted to us or to third parties for this; only your settings for them (on or off, times) are saved with your account by fin, so that they apply on all your devices. You switch both off individually in fin under Account & Settings → Notifications and set their times there.
