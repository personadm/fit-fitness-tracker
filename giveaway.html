Story Giveaway

Last updated: 03.10.2026 DRAFT, legal review

This is a translation. If the English and the German version differ, the German version applies.

In short

  • Add your Instagram username in fin. You get one entry for free, without posting anything.
  • For every further entry: post a fin workout, the fin tile or your Live Activity (Android: fin tile or notification) publicly to your Instagram story and tag @finfitnesstracker.
  • At most one story entry per day. Only story entries we have confirmed count.
  • Free. No purchase necessary, fin Pro does not improve your chances.
  • Apple, Google and Instagram are not involved.
  • No cash alternative.

2. Period and draw

The giveaway runs for 30 days, from 04.10.2026, 00:00 to 02.11.2026, 23:59 (German time). Every day follows German time (Europe/Berlin), not the clock of your device. For a story, the day it was posted according to Instagram counts. The status “being checked” after you tap “Share” in fin is set by the clock of our server.

We draw the winners on 05.11.2026.

While the giveaway runs, fin shows it on the home screen and when you share a workout or the fin tile. There you open it, add your Instagram username and see how many entries you have. At times another offer may appear before it on the home screen.

3. Who can take part

(1) Taking part is free. You do not have to buy anything. A subscription or purchase of fin Pro does not improve your chances.

(2) You can take part if you are at least 16 years old and live in Germany, Austria or Switzerland. If fin knows your age from your profile and it is below that, you cannot take part. If fin does not know it, you confirm that you are old enough when you add your Instagram username.

If you are under 18, you need the consent of a parent or guardian to take part and to accept the prize.

(3) You need a fin account and an Instagram account. For entries from stories the Instagram account must be public. One fin account and one Instagram account count per person.

(4) The organizer, people involved in organizing the giveaway and their relatives cannot take part.

4. How an entry is made

One entry for free. When you add your Instagram username in fin, you have one entry without posting anything. It counts in the draw like any other entry. Each person gets it once.

(1) Every further entry is a public Instagram story that

  • shows a fin workout (the workout card from fin), the fin tile from the home screen (the image from fin) or your fin Live Activity (on Android: the fin tile or the fin notification) and
  • in which @finfitnesstracker is tagged.

You do not need a giveaway graphic of your own. Please do not post one.

(2) At most one story entry per day. If you post several stories on one day, one of them counts. Over the whole period you can collect at most as many story entries as the giveaway has days, plus the free one. That is at most 30 story entries.

(3) Only public stories count. We cannot see stories for “Close Friends” or stories from private accounts, so they do not count.

(4) Do not delete the story before it disappears on its own after 24 hours. Otherwise we may not find it.

(5) Your Instagram username must be added in fin (in the giveaway, see section 2). Only stories from the day you added the username count. We use it to match your story to your fin account. Each username can be added by one fin account only. You can change it until your first story is confirmed; after that it stays. Only add your own username.

(6) When you tap “Share” in fin, fin shows your entry for today as “being checked”. Tapping alone is not an entry. It counts once we have found and confirmed the story. You do not have to tap: a matching story also counts if you posted it directly in Instagram.

5. How we check entries

(1) Once or twice a day the organizer looks at the story mentions of @finfitnesstracker and records them as a screen recording. An entry is confirmed only after the organizer has checked the story in the recording.

(2) Of the story entries, only confirmed ones count. The free entry always counts. In fin you can see for every entry whether it is confirmed, still being checked or was not found.

(3) If Instagram does not show us a mention, for example because the story was not public, the tag was missing, the story was deleted early or Instagram did not report it, we cannot confirm the entry. If you posted a story that is missing, write to us within 2 days at support@fin-tracker.de or by message to @finfitnesstracker.

6. Prizes

(1) You can win:

  • 3 Gymshark vouchers worth €100 each
  • 3 Prozis vouchers worth €50 each
  • 3 × fin Pro annual subscription (12 months of fin Pro)

(2) We buy the vouchers ourselves and send them digitally. Their redemption is subject to the terms of Gymshark and Prozis, including validity and countries. Gymshark and Prozis are not organizers of this giveaway.

(3) We unlock the fin Pro annual subscription for the fin account you used to take part: 12 months of fin Pro, without payment. After that it ends on its own and does not renew. It is not a purchase in the App Store or on Google Play and therefore cannot be restored or refunded through the store.

(4) If you already have fin Pro, you still get the annual subscription. The 12 months start when your paid period ends. A running subscription does not end by itself: cancel it in the App Store or on Google Play, otherwise you keep paying.

(5) No cash payment, no exchange for other prizes and no transfer of the prize to another person.

7. Draw and notification

(1) After the giveaway ends we draw the winners at random from all entries: the free entry of every participant and the confirmed story entries. Every entry counts the same: more entries mean a higher chance. Each person can win only once. The order of the draw decides who gets which prize: the first three drawn get a Gymshark voucher each, the next three a Prozis voucher each, the next three a fin Pro annual subscription each.

(2) We notify winners by message from @finfitnesstracker to the Instagram account they added and by a notification in fin to the fin account they used. We never ask for passwords, payment details or fees.

(3) To make sure that the Instagram account and the fin account belong to you, the notification in fin contains a code. Send it to @finfitnesstracker within 7 days by message from exactly the Instagram account you added. We also check that you have reached the minimum age. For a voucher we need an email address to send it to.

(4) If you do not reply in time, if we cannot confirm that the account is yours, or if you do not meet these rules, the prize is forfeited. We then draw a new winner from the remaining entries.

(5) We only name a winner's Instagram username publicly with their consent.

8. Exclusion

We can void entries and exclude participants, also afterwards and after the draw, if they break these rules or manipulate the giveaway. Examples: adding another person's Instagram username, passing off other people's stories as your own, using several fin or Instagram accounts, giving a false age or using automated tools. A prize already awarded can then be reclaimed.

9. Early end and changes

We may end, pause or change the giveaway early for good reason, for example if it can no longer run properly for technical or legal reasons, if Instagram no longer shows the mentions or if we detect manipulation. If it ends early, we draw among the entries that exist until then, where possible and fair. We only change these rules during the giveaway where necessary and without putting participants at a disadvantage.

10. Privacy

(1) The controller is the organizer from section 1. Otherwise our Privacy Policy applies.

(2) What we process: your Instagram username, your fin account ID, your entries (day, time of tapping “Share”, status of the check), your confirmation of the minimum age and, if stored in your profile, your age to check the minimum age. For winners also the details of the draw (rank, prize, number of entries), the code for the proof and the contact details we need to send the prize.

(3) Recordings of the mentions. The screen recordings of the story mentions show the Instagram usernames and stories of everyone who tagged @finfitnesstracker. We use them only to confirm entries and delete them after the evaluation, at the latest after 7 days. Only mentions of Instagram usernames added in fin are evaluated; we skip all others and put them on no list. We therefore do not notify people who do not take part individually (Art. 14(5)(b) GDPR). If a story shows health details, such as heart rate or calories, the person has made them public themselves (Art. 9(2)(e) GDPR).

(4) Service providers. To evaluate the recordings we use technical service providers as processors under Art. 28 GDPR, including providers based in the USA. A person confirms every story entry. PLEASE CONFIRM: data processing agreement and basis for the transfer to the USA are in place

(5) The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because we need the data to run the giveaway under these rules. For mentions by people who do not take part we rely on our legitimate interest in checking the entries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); we do not use these details otherwise.

(6) Deletion. We delete Instagram usernames and entries 30 days after the draw and the handover of all prizes. We keep the details of the winners as proof of the draw until possible claims are time-barred, at most 3 years. If you delete your fin account, your Instagram username and your entries are gone immediately; you then no longer take part in the draw.

(7) You can stop taking part at any time. Write to support@fin-tracker.de and we delete your Instagram username and your entries. Your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and complaint are described in the Privacy Policy.

(8) What Instagram does with your story is up to Meta under its own terms and privacy policy.

Apple, Google and Instagram are not involved. The giveaway is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Apple, Google or Instagram (Meta Platforms). The only contact for all questions about the giveaway is the organizer.

By taking part you release Instagram from any liability in connection with this giveaway. Your stories are also subject to Instagram's Terms of Use and Community Guidelines.

12. Liability

We are not liable for disruptions at Instagram, on the network or on your device that stop a story from reaching us. This does not affect our liability for intent and gross negligence and for damage from injury to life, body or health.

13. Applicable law

German law applies. If you are a consumer with your habitual residence in another country, you keep the mandatory consumer protection rules of that country.

If a provision of these rules is invalid, the others remain valid.
